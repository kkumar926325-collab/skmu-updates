Privacy Policy

SKMU Updates is an unofficial, student-run app that shares admission, result, form, exam and notice information related to SKMU Dumka. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the university.

What we collect

  • Name and question text that you type when you ask a question on a post. These are visible to other users of the app.
  • A random device ID generated inside the app (not linked to your name, phone number or account) used only to count how many people use the app.
  • Basic server logs (such as IP address and time of request) kept by the hosting server for security and abuse prevention.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, phone number, email, photos or files from your device. We do not show ads and we do not sell data.

Notifications

The app can show a notification when a new update is posted. You can turn notifications off in your phone settings at any time.

Questions and moderation

Questions can be reported by users. The admin may delete questions or posts that are abusive, misleading or inappropriate.

Data deletion

You can ask for your questions to be deleted by contacting us at the address below.

Contact

Contact details are shown in the app (Info tab).
